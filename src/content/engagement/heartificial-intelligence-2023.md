An installation made by the artist group TripleDotMakers, the ECHO Teens (a group of young people with congenital heart conditions and their siblings), and researchers from the School of Biomedical Engineering and Imaging Sciences. It asked whether AI can see a patient as a person and not only as data. Visitors placed a finger on a heart-rate sensor and watched hearts drawn by the ECHO Teens pulse with their own heartbeat.
